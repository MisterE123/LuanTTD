# Homogeneous multi-wagon industrial rail

Reference: pinned OpenTTD 16.0-RC1.

Pinned OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 `economy.cpp`: `ReserveConsist` walks eligible physical vehicles; `LoadUnloadVehicle` scans bodies in stock order with each one's capacity and load slice, records cargo masks by CargoID, and shares the front vehicle's timer/payment. `UpdateLoadUnloadTicks` applies one 40-tick train loading pulse and any length overhang once. `PrepareUnload` / `CargoPayment` accumulate real delivery/transfer routes until settlement. `Train::GetWeight`, `GroundVehicle::CargoChanged` and `CargoSpec::WeightOfNUnitsInTrain` calculate physical wagon cargo weight separately before consist accumulation. Train route/terminal positions use the existing source-ported native pathfinding/reversal.

Start with a modeled conventional locomotive and up to two genuine stock #35 Iron Ore Hoppers, full load at existing Iron Ore Mine platform, unload/no load at its Steel Mill destination platform. No settings switch. Depot purchase and yard attachment are genuine stock; one engine/one hopper remains supported. Other freight combinations, additional engines, incompatible wagon types, longer freight formations, and stops too short for this slice remain excluded. Reuse shared ordered body reserve/pulse/leave and the existing industry production/acceptance/staging pipeline, store one unique per-visit receipt sequence, and keep separate wagon packets, age, actual tiles and loaded meshes with stable identities. Test acquisition, departure with both loaded, physical differently loaded wagons, SQLite restarts before and after payout, per-wagon weight and stock-shaped cash, and native single-freight/mixed-town regressions.

## Play and verify

When the ordinary world has mounted the Iron Ore Mine, Steel Mill and stock #35 art, buy the two-hopper formation directly or buy the locomotive and both hoppers separately in the Train Yard. Designate the two freight stages through Rail Freight → Iron and steel trains. Use platforms at least two tiles long and assign Iron Ore Mine loading then Steel Mill unloading. The conventional engine is purchased from its actual available roster. There is no enable setting.

- [Pinned source](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/tree/16.0-RC1/src): `economy.cpp` `CargoPayment::PayFinalDelivery` (1218), `PrepareUnload` (1256), `GetLoadAmount` (1299), `ReserveConsist` (1566), `UpdateLoadUnloadTicks` (1596), `LoadUnloadVehicle` (1614), `LoadUnloadStation` (1929); `table/engines.h` stock #35; `train.h` `Train::GetWeight` (236), `ground_vehicle.cpp` `GroundVehicle::CargoChanged` (79) and `cargotype.cpp` `CargoSpec::WeightOfNUnitsInTrain` (259).
- `lua5.1 tests/stock_rail_multi_ore_service.lua` verifies independent 5 t per-body slices with one 40-tick full-consist timer, 10 ticks without gradual loading, and the one-tile stock helper case (160 ticks when the 24-unit train overhangs 8 units, once per visit). It also verifies twelve unique wagon drops, one industry scan per active station/tick, and one cash payment. Native multi-wagon dispatch deliberately refuses the one-tile platform rather than presenting an under-length journey as supported.
- `bash tests/stock_rail_multi_ore_live_native.sh` builds real rail/depot/platforms, refuses short or unsupported stock arrangements, buys genuine #35s individually, checks actual per-wagon posed IDs and ore meshes (21 t and 0 t), then reopens SQLite and observes both fully loaded wagons moving. A second reopen restores an unpaid $199/10 t partial unload. One settlement pays exactly $1,199 for 60 t with company cash $278,882; a final reopen verifies no re-credit. Single #35/#36 and mixed Coach/Mail rail remain independent.
- This is not unrestricted freight composition: other cargoes remain single-wagon, the Train Yard can store assemblies that are not dispatchable, and there is still one service per industrial cargo. Generalized freight supports neither mixed cargo, longer running formations, nor under-length stations, additional engines, refits or automatic cargo routing.
