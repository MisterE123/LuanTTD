# Street-level sightseeing

Use **Survey → Walk** to land on an aimed street, open ground, bridge deck
or water. Use **Survey → Drive Car** to enter a private car on a connected
road. When standing on a connected road in Walk, **Inventory → Drive**
gets into a car without leaving street view; leaving the car returns to
Walk. Walking and running use the pedestrian model's scale with a 1.5×
sightseeing pace. Hold **E** (Luanti's Aux1 key) to run. Swimming gets the
same 1.5× horizontal boost; surfacing still uses Jump. Normal Luanti zoom
works in every scenic view. Right-click does not switch modes.

From Walk, aim at a nearby passenger vehicle and use the primary action.
Trains offer an explicit passenger seat or driver's cab. Buses, passenger
ferries and passenger aircraft use a window seat. Looking around leaves
the seat attached, and the ride turns along with the vehicle rather than
taking control of the cursor. Only the shell of the occupied car or cab
is replaced, so the rest of the train stays visible through the windows.
Use **Inventory → Leave ride** to step onto a nearby platform, dock or
street when one is safe. If there's no safe landing nearby, stay aboard
and try again as the vehicle approaches a stop.

There is one other way to go sightseeing from Walk: open Country and City
airport hangars have a parked aircraft in a side bay. The aircraft in the bay
is interactive, not a commercial vehicle or a flight tool on the hotbar.

Bridge guardrails and farm and house fences have collision. Rails, level
crossings and raised station platforms have walking surfaces. Dock ramps
and decks are walkable with an open boarding end. In water, use the movement
controls to swim and hold jump to surface and reach a bank.

At supported single-tile building entrances one level above a straight street,
seven outward curbside steps connect the street to the existing building plot.
They do not cut into the building or change the road. A stop on the frontage
tile suppresses the steps; a stop beside the approach reverses them if the
opposite end is clear.

**Controls → decorative scenery** shows drifting voxel cloud banks, low mist,
showers, and thunderstorms. Banks follow the terrain. A storm grows from an
existing cloud and settles back into it as the rain stops. Nearby clouds ease
aside for builders, but stay in place for walking, riding, flying, vehicle follow,
and cinematic views. This setting is per player. Weather has no effects on
vehicle operations, construction, or the simulation.

Country Airfield windsocks stream downwind and ripple in the breeze.
They follow the prevailing cloud drift with small local variation.
This is visual weather; it does not affect aircraft handling.
