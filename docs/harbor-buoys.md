# Harbor buoys

Open **Sea & Air → Harbors → Waterworks**. **Place public buoy** costs
$350 and needs an open, flat water tile with no bridge above it. The
marker is public. **Remove buoy, keep water** costs $50 and preserves
the sea, river or canal beneath it, including canal ownership. A
buoy occupied by a vessel or referred to by an active order cannot
be removed.

Open **Harbors → Ferry Operations → Order ferry**. Click your ship
depot, your first dock, the public buoy, and your second dock. Skip
the buoy for a direct route. After leaving the first dock, the ferry
uses the marker in both directions. It aims at the buoy, then advances
the order within three tiles of it. The ship does not stop, load or
earn a fare at the marker. Clear the current ferry before assigning
another one or changing its order. Right-click cancels a selection
that hasn't been dispatched.

This ports OpenTTD 14.1's flat-water build and removal checks,
YAPF destination and ShipController waypoint handling. The preview
supports one optional marker on the passenger ferry, not a
multi-buoy freight order list.

The float, fittings and daymark are original 3D geometry.
The editable Blender source and native mesh generator are in the Git bundle.
