# LuanTTD native QA: first bus route

**Baseline:** Harbor Buoys standalone, d1cbd6d.
**Method:** Opened the real Linux Luanti 5.10 client with a fresh profile and a
disposable default TerraGenesis seed 1751 save. Operated the world with actual
keyboard, mouse and formspecs. Main path: generate a world, navigate tools,
place stops and a depot, buy a Regal, select two destinations, and start service.
No gameplay fixture or shortcut built the route. Only one town and one bus
were covered in this pass; this is not a claim of release-wide UX coverage.

## Findings and fixes in this QA build

| Priority | What happened | Change |

| High | Entering Roadworks on key 2 silently carried the selection to Bulldoze. The next ordinary click on a house removed it and charged $320. | When a menu change lands on Bulldoze, it is not armed. The first press only arms it. A separate press starts work; purposefully selecting Bulldoze still works normally. |
| High | The initial guide told me to build between two towns. The opening camera showed one town with a usable local street loop. | The first route guide now starts with two roadside stops on existing streets and a depot next to that network. | High | The stop menu said a straight road was required, while its green preview offered a paid stop on an empty tile. Depot previews show entrance direction. |
| Medium | The tutorial said “Go to…” while the actual vehicle button said “Add stops.” The “2 stops saved” receipt landed on the Full load button. | The tutorial names the actual button. The receipt has a separate row. The Control desk now links straight to Controls. |
| Medium | The first-run “Generate world” button actually saved settings and ended the setup session. The player must reopen the same world. | The button now says “Save and return”; the form explains exactly when generation happens. “Cancel setup” exits without saving options. ## Replay and remaining work

- The first full held click on inherited Bulldoze left a valid stop intact;
  a separate intentional click removed it at its quoted $80. A normal flight
  and demolition regression also passed the diagonal modifier.
- The revised preview showed *Unconnected bus stop* on bare ground and
  *Through bus stop* on a town street; a depot quote showed the entrance compass
  direction. Two through stops and a depot were placed in the real campaign.
  The bought Regal picked both orders, started service, and paid a passenger fare.
- Town labels, stop labels and the floating fare still overlap in a busy block,
  especially at street zoom. This needs a separate label-priority pass.
- The order picker marks valid stops but does not highlight the drivable path
  between them before starting service. A non-destructive route preview would
  catch most dead-end and depot connection mistakes earlier. No path preview is
  claimed in this QA build.

## Follow-up: all transport modes in native Luanti

The first bus pass was too narrow. This follow-up used the Linux Luanti
5.10 client with disposable generated campaigns (TerraGenesis seed 1751).
Construction, purchases and order picks used the actual game UI. The
built-in Survey command sometimes positioned the camera for repeatable
aiming. No other player's save was used.

| Mode | What the playtest actually did | Result and finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Road passenger | Built two through stops and a depot; bought a Regal; picked two orders and started service. | Saw a $10 passenger fare in the first pass. The road orders overlay still uses Pick and Done; service is started from the vehicle window. |
| Road freight | Built a 23-tile AutoRoad trunk and turnaround loops, placed forest/sawmill stops and a depot, bought and dispatched a Witcombe Wood Truck. | Delivered 160 t for $1,824 gross. Bare-ground truck-stop quotes now warn that they are unconnected. Right-click opens the freight stop's cargo information. Coal and the other road cargo chains were not rerun. |

The shared order hotbar now stages the second platform and requires Dispatch. ### First train recipe (updated)

Build one connected line with two platforms near houses and a depot.
Buy a passenger consist, choose its two platform orders and dispatch.
An isolated, simple service no longer requires a path signal; the train
reverses when the next order is in the other direction. The earlier
signal workaround described a port gap, not an OpenTTD requirement.

The shared order hotbar is not a route preview: eligible-highlighted
destinations are valid objects, but reachability, signals and route
restrictions are still checked at Dispatch. The physical Pick slot
can move when the engine preserves the previously selected key;
follow the key shown in the overlay. World labels and route feedback
can still crowd at low resolutions. The Fleet menu exposes **Road
vehicles**, not a unified fleet manager. This is a QA preview,
not all of OpenTTD and not a Windows runtime verification.
