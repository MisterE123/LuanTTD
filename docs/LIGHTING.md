# Lighting

The regular sky follows the campaign calendar: one sun/moon circuit per
simulation year, including leap years. **Controls → Daylight** switches between
**Cycle** (default) and **Always day**. Always day holds the native sun and its
shadows in the morning; the calendar, vehicles, economy, and town growth
keep running. Restoring Cycle shows the current phase, not the phase at which
you switched. Single-player and multiplayer operators can change this setting.
It applies to everyone in the world and is saved with that world.

LuanTTD supplies a subtle bloom and 20% strength for Luanti's built-in
volumetric lighting (godrays). Enable **Shaders**, **Post Processing**,
**Bloom**, and **Volumetric Lighting** in Luanti's graphics settings to draw
the effect. This is separate from Dynamic Shadows, which remains optional.
Clients without support or with post processing disabled still get the same
sun, sky and day/night behavior. The game does not change your global client
graphics settings.
