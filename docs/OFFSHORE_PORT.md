# Offshore production and rotary flight

The source target is [OpenTTD 16.0-RC1](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/tree/16.0-RC1), `07b339c`. Normal generated campaigns can open new supported industries as the economy progresses. Oil Rigs have no initial map-generation weight, so even a game started in 1965 does not open with a rig. Starting in 1960 the stock probability enters the builder; a rig needs a clear suitable site near the map edge. Use its neutral berth for crude tankers. Buy a Tricario at a Country or City Airport and order it to the rig for passenger and Mail service. The rig itself has no depot; a fixed-wing aircraft cannot use it.

## Reference and evidence

- `industry_cmd.cpp`: `CheckNewIndustry_OilRig`, `CheckIfIndustryTilesAreFree`, `MakeIndustryTileBigger`, `PopulateStationsNearby`, `IndustryBuildData` and the daily industry timer. `station_cmd.cpp`: `BuildOilRig` converts the first GFX 24 into an unowned station, with separate ship and airport facilities. The water class survives removal. The six structure cells are not a rectangular filled buffer.
- `table/build_industry.h`: layout 0, temperate appear weight 6 from 1960, initial-world weight 0, oil/passenger rates 15/2, minimum 5, and conflict with a refinery 14 tiles away or closer. GFX 24 accepts 8/8 passengers; GFX 25 accepts 8/8 Mail, but the rig does not produce Mail.
- `airport.cpp`, `table/airport_{movement,defaults}.h`, `aircraft_cmd.cpp`: one neutral oil-rig helicopter pad, no hangar, 54px landing-height offset, descend by station-anchor ground sample, keep the rig's original station tile on the actual apron outside that tile. Neutral status affects use and catchment; it is not a grant of ownership or construction access.
- `tests/oil_rig_upstream.py /path/to/openttd-16.0-RC1 lua5.1` compares every footprint/buffer row and the whole flight table and block graph. `tests/industry_build_scheduler_upstream.py` with the same arguments compiles the actual RC1 builder functions and checks 1,704 checkpoints on 64², 256×512 and 4096² with every RNG draw, target count, wake state and notice.
- `python3 tests/rotary_motion_upstream.py /path/to/openttd-16.0-RC1 lua5.1` compiles the original rotary tick and both vertical branches; all 1,220 fixtures agree, including blind landing, anchor sampling, sound trigger and stopped/hidden cases.
- `lua5.1 tests/offshore_rig_world.lua` records atomically unmodified stale plans, correct rates/minimum shipment, one neutral ID, exact berths and water restoration. This does not yet run in a normal game.
- `lua5.1 tests/oil_rig_rules.lua`, `lua5.1 tests/oil_rig_helicopter.lua` and [the original art tests](../art/stock_oil_rig.md) exercise clearance, pad holds and the native models.

## Ordinary qualification

- `lua5.1 tests/industry_openings_runtime.lua` checks actual opening state, 1960 gating, precise stock RNG draw order and Funded Only. A real generated campaign replay also advances the ordinary worker from a stock oil-rig target quota, chooses the offshore location with the real RNG, and proves its Gazette headline and neutral station through a mid-build remount. Native ground layout is still constrained to supported mesh variants.
- `bash tests/stock_offshore_tanker_worker_native.sh` serves a real constructed rig from the normal worker. Its tanker orders are available by destination list and direct selection. The fixture saved with 10 kL aboard, reopened and paid for 220 kL at the refinery, then reopened a third time without duplicate credit.
- `lua5.1 tests/offshore_rotary_worker.lua` and its `city` variant exercise both airfield layouts and real rolled-forward stock cargo. `bash tests/offshore_rotary_worker_native.sh` repeats with native processes and SQLite restart. The stocked outbound (people and Mail) flight returns with offshore workers.
- `bash tests/stock_oil_rig_art_native.sh` checks actual visual assets at sea level and from the air, including the two animated attached rotors on their intended deck.
