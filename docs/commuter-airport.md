# Commuter Airport

Available from **1983** under **Airfields → Metro & Commuter Airports**.
It uses the OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 **5×4** site, radius-four catchment, three
stands, single hangar, and shared short runway. Long-runway aircraft
cannot buy or start a route from this field. Helicopters can approach
separately and choose an available pad.

The test campaign built and painted all 20 cells, bought a Yate Aerospace
YAE46, watched taxi, takeoff and arrival, and checked that the service
still moved after restarting. The ordinary paid-simulation tests also
operate a Tricario helicopter between a Commuter and City airport.
[Editable Blender art](../art/commuter_airport_parts.blend) covers the
tower, fenced apron and fenced pads. Shared source graphic roles retain
the existing native assets. Noise accounting is still not mounted.
