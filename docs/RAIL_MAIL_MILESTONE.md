# Ordinary stock Rail Mail (#28)

Reference: pinned OpenTTD 16.0-RC1.

## Source behavior and intended ownership

- `table/engines.h` conventional ID 28: 21 t tare, 30 mail bags, image index 35, buy factor 228 ($1,781 base), 8 length units, five bags/load pulse; standard freight multiplier does not apply to Mail. `table/cargo_const.h`: cargo ID 2, weight 4/16 t per bag, payment 4550, transit periods 20 and 90, road/tram unit multiplier 0x200 (not a multiplier for the conventional train's 30 stock wagon capacity). `table/train_sprites.h` stock base sprite 2741, four headings, no load variant, existing editable (art/rail_mail_van.blend).
- `town_cmd.cpp` TownGenerateCargo issues one production event per completed house/cargo on the real tile loop. `station_cmd.cpp` MoveGoodsToStation chooses physical stations together after catchment, select_goods, rights, and StationID-ordered eligibility. Rail and road therefore share one source callback and one fixed-point allotment. The Road-only fallback keeps its behavior if the Mail Van model/service is off.
- The ordinary Rail Mail train uses the same bought locomotive, real depot, station, orders, stock train movement, reservation, running charges and clock as the paid town rail shuttle. No new rail stage and no cloned railway timetable. A legal initial consist is one ordinary modeled locomotive and one #28. Ordinary town platforms can host both passengers and Mail, with separate queue/rating/acceptance and one station loading-order scan and station rating phase. Explicit industry stages remain reserved for their designated cargo. Mixed coach/Mail consists, aircraft Mail and DMU Mail are separate gates.
- Persist Mail as a single physical cargo body with explicit unit count, real wagon-tile packet distance, weight and speed, 185-tick stock age, and load/unload payment receipt. Accept only where the current house Mail eighths cross 8; use original always-accepted detail rather than pretending Mail is industrial cargo or pays at the source. Use the existing town-associated receipt and company/purchased-vehicle running money. Mail bags never become crash passengers, passenger-crowd population or passenger revenue labels. Toggling/rewriting orders must not reset an existing visit.
- Stock source cross-checks: `station_cmd.cpp` acceptance/rating callbacks, road truck vs rail catchment; `economy.cpp` per-body LoadUnloadVehicle, GetTransportedGoodsIncome, DeliverGoods/per-company credit; `cargopacket.cpp` reservation, transfer, origin and age accounting; `train_cmd.cpp` vehicle cargo-aging and actual vehicle position/cargo cache.

## Proof gates

1. Exact source roster, asset and availability tests. #28 remains not purchasable when original model/atlas or compatible worker service is absent; no reuse of #28 for another cargo.
2. Deterministic real town source competition with a road mail truck and rail Mail Van covering the same emitting house; assert fractional conservation and that a bus-only station never gets Mail. Verify both loading/rating independently and per-platform overlapping construction footprint acceptance, including one multiple-house fixture where 7/8 fails and 8/8 succeeds.
3. Same platform with competing paid passenger and Mail trains: shared stock loading scan but separate queues, company/member ledgers and town receipt/crowds. Verify incompatible industry platforms, interstation order guards, stop/edit and repeat behavior; no dual-cargo consists promoted unless fully verified.
4. Run actual Luanti worker and client: buy #28 through native depot assembly, conditional cargo/picker tip, ordinary owned orders and live modeled departure; record an authentic partial load, stop/reopen SQLite, deliver real Mail once with expected age/distance/fare and running costs, reproduce cash. Recheck existing paid passenger, industry rail and road Mail tests. Record evidence locations.

## Implementation and QA checkpoint

- The public-to-the-game entry points are the real depot assembly or standard depot purchase, targeting two ordinary owned rail platforms, and the normal vehicle order window. Inspect a town rail platform to see pending Mail, actual acceptance and station rating. Pickup is not conditional on the station accepting deliveries.
- Each physical rail station's paid-town host owns one station small-tick phase and ordered loading queue. Passenger and Mail each keep their own GoodsEntry rating, acceptance and packets. Mail uses the depot-purchased wagon's actual tile and the shared running-money ledger, without assigning Mail bags to the passenger crowd or crash victims. A checked-catchment reverse index keeps the town emitter bounded by the stations that actually cover this house.
- `tests/stock_rail_mail_service.lua` verifies exact base roster data and missing/mixed availability guards, 7/8 versus 8/8 acceptance, unfinished-house acceptance, one stock fractional road/rail contest, both passenger and Mail loading at the same platforms, phase/age, no origin delivery, wagon-tile distance and exact Mail payment.
- `tests/stock_rail_mail_live_native.sh` authors each physical rail/road depot and line from ordinary worker commands. Two companies serve overlapping postal catchments. The worker's first process persisted 5 genuine bags, rail actor identity and mass; the fresh SQLite process restored the age phase, delivered 30 bags for $576, charged operating costs and reconciled exact company cash. Real MPS Mail Truck deliveries and Mail-only town receipts were checked separately. This also verifies the authoritative Rail Mail inspection while the clock is paused.
- Reused original editable Rail Mail artwork rather than producing a replacement or a loaded texture for its stock fixed appearance. Coupled Kirby and #28 rendered in an actual Linux client on straight and curved native rail; independent native worker actor projection selected the Mail Van model.

Out of scope deliberately: consists carrying both Mail and passengers, more than one Mail Van per consist, aircraft Mail, linked cargo distribution, and coupling/decoupling en route. The next safe mixed-consist milestone must preserve part-level FIFO, transfer and per-vehicle cash when cargo types share a station, not emulate another train actor. This branch is private and has not been pushed.
