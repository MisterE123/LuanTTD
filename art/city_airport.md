# City Airport (AT_LARGE)

Original Blender recreation of the OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 6×6 City Airport. It has three landside offices, a stepped round Art Deco terminal with three pier branches, an open maintenance hangar, air traffic control tower, braced radio mast, radar and perimeter fencing. The apron includes physical pavement, three stock hold marks, runway and landing lights set flush with the surface, and a separate passenger waiting terrace.

## Use

- 36 meshes: `mods/luanttd_core/models/bl_city_airport_{x}_{z}.obj` with 0–5 coordinates.
- Shared atlas: `mods/luanttd_core/textures/bl_city_airport_atlas.png`.
- Native flat placement: `(origin_x + 2*x, ground_y + 1, origin_z + 2*z)`. Tile type, FTA, activity, continuous terrain and orientation remain the game runtime's responsibility.
- Native FTA hold centers: stand 1 `(2.75, 4.625)`, stand 2 `(6.5, 2.25)`, stand 3 `(4.25, .5)`. Takeoff and landing runway paths are `z=10.125` and `z=10.375`.
- The depot is stock tile `(5,0)` at `(10.125,-.125)`. The hangar opens toward increasing Z and its swept-clearance western side is cut across adjoining apron `(4,0)`. Neither the cantilevered west hangar bay nor the elevated east boarding pier requires changing a stock aircraft waypoint.
- The independent ground passenger waiting terrace lies within local X `1.78–2.55`, Z `.04–.78`, at Y `ground+.026`. All its protective furniture remains outside this area.

## Build and verify

```sh
blender -b -t 3 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/build_city_airport_art.py
python tests/city_airport_art.py
python tests/city_airport_art.py --airframe mods/luanttd_core/models/bl_bakewell_cotswald.obj 1
```

Set `CITY_AIRPORT_REVIEW` to an existing output directory for four optional Blender stills. In a copied staging directory, set `LUANTTD_HELPERS` to the game checkout's `tools` and `LUANTTD_BASE` to the checkout root. The optional motion test uses the available Lua runtime via `LUA` or the platform Lua 5.4 library.

The model has 27,972 triangles across 36 tile files. The test verifies all native placement and runway joins, the exact stand markers and passenger area, source pins when supplied, and ability to clear structures in 3,961 stock and interpolated ground poses generated by the native City FTA across all three arrival/stand/depot paths. This does not evaluate vehicle-to-vehicle intersections or prove world mounting.
