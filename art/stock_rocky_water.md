# Temperate rocky water and rapids

OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 `src/water_cmd.cpp` draws `ClearRocks` over the
usual water class and `CoastRocks` over the usual shoreline; both use
the slope's rock overlay. The native art uses the pinned shore masks
`1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9,11,12,13,14,23,27,29,30` and level open sea.
It does not put rocks on all water or change the shape, waterline,
coast clearance, or any world-generation probabilities.

- **Rocky shore:** an exact copy of the ordinary tapered land/shingle
  and submerged bank, with the original texture pixels in a combined
  atlas. Three to six faceted stone pieces follow the existing planar
  terrain. Rocks near or across the waterline embed into the bank.
- **Open-water rocks:** a sculpted low skerry group over the same
  independent, waved sea as ordinary tiles. Its roots extend below
  the native wave amplitude. Ship and terrain rules are independent
  of the visual asset.
- **River rapids:** optional native decoration for the four stock
  graded river faces. Side rocks and interrupted, downhill froth
  sit on the exact flowing surface, extend no farther than one tile,
  and do not touch the animated river texture or hydrology. There
  is no special upstream rocky-river tile class.

The OBJ assets and palettes are generated by
`tools/build_stock_rocky_water_art.py`. Its `--blend` mode writes the
editable `stock_rocky_water.blend` with every rock and foam accent as
a separately named mesh. The sheet's water planes are review-only
datums. No original OpenTTD or OpenGFX sprites, pixels, or meshes were
copied. The native geometry and atlas regression is
`python3 tests/stock_rocky_water_art.py`.
