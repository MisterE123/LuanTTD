# Fences on stock split railway foundations

This extends the [existing Temperate rail fences](stock_rail_ground_fence.md) to the split and steep foundations already playable in ordinary campaigns. The rail ground state is still chosen by [OpenTTD RC1's ordinary landscape tick](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/blob/16.0-RC1/src/rail_cmd.cpp#L2739-L2776). That includes the exception for a neighboring purchased plot owned by the railway's company.

- The [stock fence shapes and diagonal height references](https://github.com/OpenTTD/OpenTTD/blob/16.0-RC1/src/rail_cmd.cpp#L1945-L2083) select a border or the corner diagonal. In 3D, the border's upright posts seat into the actual cut-slope surface. Wet upper halves retain their dedicated corner fence. Plain, brand-new split foundations show bare gravel/soil until the regular grass tick.
- Sixty reusable original meshes cover all 108 reachable dry ground/profile combinations. Native nodes for the fence and the foundation are genuinely independent, even if their elevations coincide. The fence remains decorative and doesn't alter availability, collision, reservations, speed, or route choice.
- Editable [stock_rail_split_fences.blend](stock_rail_split_fences.blend) includes all original steel frames and their packed lattice palette. Build the runtime files with `python3 tools/build_rail_split_fences.py` and `python3 tools/build_rail_split_atlas.py`. Reassemble editable art with `blender -b --python tools/save_rail_split_fences_blend.py`.
- [Exhaustive ground/profile mesh test](../tests/rail_split_fence_art.lua) checks all native forms and actual feet elevations. In the [native replay](../tests/rail_split_fence_native.lua) an owned rail curve starts on bare dirt, grows a single correctly sloped boundary beside an owned plot, and switches to the diagonal after selling the plot. The separate multiple-corner coast replay proves the upper wet and dry fence modes still mount.
