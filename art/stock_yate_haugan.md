# Yate Haugan (#218)

An original narrow-bodied fast aircraft with a swept delta wing, four individually recessed air intakes and nozzles, ten deployed wheels, and a drooping ground nose. Flight uses the clean tucked-gear model. The original OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 table supplies the stock name and dated specs, not graphics. The aircraft has 100 seats, 20 Mail bags, a 2316 km/h stock maximum and a $205,078 base price. Normal operation uses a City Airport. The existing simulation does not yet offer stock fast-aircraft crashes on short strips.

The editable Blender scene and two OBJ variants are generated by `blender -b -t 3 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/build_stock_yate_haugan_art.py`. Set `HAUGAN_REVIEW` for apron and ventral renders. Each model has an independent stock vehicle indicator and flies a real paid schedule. A swept clearance test checked 2,302 native City ground and eased intermediate poses against 36 real airport tiles in 21 height slices; the shortened pointed tip clears an outside service cabinet without moving the stock taxi path.

- `tests/yate_haugan_art.py --engines /path/to/OpenTTD-16.0-RC1/src/table/engines.h`: exact MA and AVI data, all four real intakes, geometry and native ground/flight envelopes.
- `LUANTTD_QA_AIRFRAME=218 LUANTTD_QA_AIR_RESTART=1 bash tests/stock_city_airport_native.sh`: ordinary game built a pair of City airports, paid the correct price, rendered the native taxi/flight/arrival models and resumed physical motion after shutdown.
- `LUANTTD_QA_PAID_AIRFRAME=218 bash tests/stock_aircraft_mail_live_native.sh`: actual paid service restored both compartments, then saved with $337 pending, paid $1,605 for 100 passengers and 20 bags, and did not duplicate payment or cargo after another restart.
