# Split rail foundations

These 40 original dry LuanTTD meshes cover 44 legal OpenTTD 16.0-RC1 standard-rail profiles that use half, anti-zigzag, steep-lower or steep-both foundations or a natural corner plateau. Some track layouts share the same foundation shape. They use the existing individual curved rail meshes, each at its stock railhead, rather than stretching the rails across a discontinuous surface.

- Editable, UV-mapped objects with packed grass and stone texture: [Blender source](stock_rail_split_foundations.blend).
- Reproduce the OBJ files from the exact Luanti height projection in the game root: `lua5.1 tools/build_rail_split_foundations.lua`. Pass `--check` to verify them without changing assets.
- Reproduce the grass-and-cut-stone atlas: `python3 tools/build_rail_split_atlas.py` with Pillow. It reuses LuanTTD's grass, bare rail ground, and the exact brick tile from flat retaining walls. Pass `--check` to compare stored pixels.
- Assemble the authored variants in Blender after rebuilding the meshes and atlas: `blender -b -t 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/save_rail_split_foundation_blend.py`.

[Geometry tests](../tests/rail_split_profile.lua) evaluate the continuous track and the integer train movement in both directions. [Native graphical QA](../tests/rail_split_native.lua) builds two paid curved rails on one ordinary generated slope and inspects separate railhead and wall meshes. Twenty-four additional wet variants clip the lower opening so the same animated ocean flows through the boat lane without a floating grass slab. [Native shoreline QA](../tests/rail_wet_native.lua) builds and bills the high rail, confirms the lower ship lane and then removes the rail back to plain shore. [Worker/routing QA](../tests/rail_half_water_world.lua) runs stock ship motion through the wet rail tile and verifies the terrain checkpoint. The same original diagonal fences stay on the high rail in the multi-corner wet and dry-out states. Other dry special-foundation fence art is not covered.

Dry split and raised single-axis foundations replace the original slope mesh, not just its surface. Their retaining faces extend from the stock railhead perimeter to the underlying solid fill level, with the flat retaining wall's exact horizontal and vertical brick scale and repeat phase. Wet profiles still expose the unchanged navigable lower half. [OBJ geometry verification](../tests/rail_retaining_shell.py) checks the sealed outline, outward face winding, and texture coordinates independently of the mesh generators.
